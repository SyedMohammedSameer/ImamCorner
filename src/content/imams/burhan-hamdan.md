Sheikh Burhan Hamdan serves as the Director of Islamic Affairs at the Islamic Center of Tucson. He leads most of the Friday khutbahs, teaches the evening reminders and the weekend youth and sisters' programs, and holds office hours for anyone in the community who would like to talk.
