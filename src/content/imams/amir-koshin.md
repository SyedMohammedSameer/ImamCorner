Sheikh Amir Koshin is a khateeb and lecturer at the Islamic Center of Tucson. He delivers khutbahs in the Friday rotation, leads the weekday Qur'an memorization circles and the early-week hadith reminders on the Five Pillars of Islam, and teaches the weekend adult Islamic studies class.
